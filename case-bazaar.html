The problem

Classifieds without the scams.

Nepali classifieds are fragmented across Facebook groups with no verification, no payment protection and no recourse. Buyers cannot tell a real seller from a fake listing, and sellers cannot prove they are real.

The product needed the breadth of a classifieds site with the trust mechanics of a marketplace: identity checks, a protected payment path, moderation and a dispute trail.

What we built

Nine categories, one trust layer.

  • Category-specific listing models (goods, vehicles, jobs, rentals, services, food) with their own fields and validation.
  • Seller verification: phone OTP plus ID review, with a visible verification state on listings.
  • Search and filters per category, featured listings, saved searches and a Nepali/English interface.
  • Escrow checkout with eSewa and Khalti, order states, refunds and a buyer-protection dispute flow.
  • Admin console for KYC review, listing moderation, category taxonomy and payment reconciliation.

Security and money

  • Registration can only assign buyer/seller roles; admin and reviewer roles are granted server-side after review.
  • Payment confirmation happens only after a server-to-server verification with the provider.
  • Uploaded media is content-validated by magic bytes and re-encoded before it is served.
  • Auth, OTP and search endpoints are rate-limited, and pagination is bounded.
