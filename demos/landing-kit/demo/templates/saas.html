The status quo

The close is slow because the numbers live in four places.

A spreadsheet, the bank, the payment processor and the accounting tool each hold part of the truth. Every month, someone rebuilds the bridge between them by hand.

01

Exports that disagree

A Stripe report, a bank CSVs and the ledger count the same month three ways. The difference is always found late.

02

Versions of the version

Close_v7_final_REAL.xlsx is a process, not a file. Nobody can say which reconciliation was signed off.

03

The same question every month

“Why is this payment unmatched?” arrives in the same thread, with the same manual lookup as last month.

Product

Reconcile once, then review the exceptions.

Ledgerline does the matching and shows its reasoning. You spend the time on the six items that need judgement, not the four hundred that do not.

Automatic matching

Bank lines match invoices by amount, counterparty and date. Suggested matches sit beside confirmed ones, never silently merged.

Exception queue

Anything unmatched lands in one list with the bank line and the invoice side by side. Resolve or assign it; the queue remembers.

Close checklist

Every step from bank feed to signed export, with the owner and timestamp. The checklist is the audit trail.

Cash view

Cash, runway and committed spend recomputed from the ledger. The chart shows what changed since the last close, not a decorative trend.

How it works

Three steps, one afternoon.

Connect the bank and the processor

Read-only connections. Ledgerline imports the last 90 days and shows what it found before writing anything.

Review the first match run

The queue shows suggested matches and the exceptions. Confirm the suggestions you agree with; the rest keep their reason.

Close and export

Finish the checklist, download the pack, and next month the checklist starts from the same place.

How long does migration take?

Most teams import the bank feed and one processor in an afternoon and run their first close the following month. Historical imports are optional; the 90-day window is enough to review the current period.

What do you need access to?

Read-only bank feeds and the payment processor. Ledgerline does not move money, so there is no payment permission to grant. The audit page lists every connection and its last read.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Export the full ledger, the reconciliation log and the attachments in standard formats. Deleting the workspace removes the data after a short grace period; the export does not require a support ticket.
