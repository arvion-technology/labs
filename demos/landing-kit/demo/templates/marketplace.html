The money

Why the payment waits.

Cash still works. The held option exists for the transactions where neither side wants to move first.

01

Buyer pays into hold

Card or wallet. The seller sees the amount held and the pickup window before leaving home.

02

Both confirm the handover

One code from the buyer, one tap from the seller. If both do not happen, the payment stays held.

03

Dispute inside 24 hours

Either side can freeze the release and open a review. The chat log and listing photos are attached automatically.

How it works

Three steps to a pickup.

Find it within your radius

Filter by category and distance. The feed shows the pickup window and whether the payment is held.

Agree a place and time

Chat in the listing, then pick a public pickup point from the suggested list. The address is shared only after both agree.

Confirm the handover

Check the item, confirm in the app, and the held payment releases to the seller.

Safety

Meeting a stranger, handled.

Pickup is public by default, the phone number stays hidden until both sides agree, and the held payment means neither side has to bring leverage to the meetup.

Start selling →
What stops a no-show?

A held payment and a pickup window. If the buyer does not turn up, the seller can release the hold as a cancellation and the buyer's rating records it. Repeat no-shows lose the held-payment option.

Why is delivery not supported?

Because the promise is local. Adding shipping means national logistics, returns and a cost structure that turns a neighbourhood marketplace into a worse version of the big platforms.

What does the 3% cover?

Payment processing and dispute review. There is no monthly subscription, and cash deals under NPR 1,000 are exempt because there is no payment to process.

Start with what is already in your cupboard.

List one item in four fields. If it does not sell in two weeks, remove it; there is no fee and no penalty.
