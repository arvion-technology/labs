Do you train on our documents?

No. Documents are indexed for retrieval and used to answer inside your workspace only. The self-hosted plan runs the same pipeline on your infrastructure.

How do permissions work?

The connector reads the source system's permission model at query time. If a document is moved to a folder the asker cannot read, its citations disappear from future answers.

What if it makes something up?

Answers are constrained to retrieved passages. When retrieval returns nothing above the relevance floor, Vela says it could not find the answer and lists the closest documents. It can still misread a passage; that is why the citation is one click away.
