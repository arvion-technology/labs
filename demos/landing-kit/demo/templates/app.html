The plan

Built for weeks that do not go to plan.

Most running apps treat a missed session as failure. Tempo treats it as information about your week.

Adaptive weeks

Miss Wednesday and Thursday becomes the easy run. The long run shrinks only if the week's total would otherwise jump too far.

Effort, not pace

Sessions are prescribed by effort and time. A hilly route in humid weather is allowed to be slower.

Routes that suit the session

Loop for the long run, an out-and-back for intervals. Tempo suggests a direction, not just a distance.

How it adapts

Three steps, then the plan runs itself.

Pick the days you can run

Four days, or three if that is honest. The plan is built around the week you have, not an ideal one.

Run the first session

No test race and no pace target. The first two weeks establish a starting volume from what you actually do.

Let the week re-plan

Miss a day and the next open slot takes the session. Change your mind again and it moves again; the history keeps both choices.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. After launch the plan is free for one active plan and a small yearly price for unlimited plan history; beta users keep their data either way.

What happens to my run data?

Runs sync to your account so the plan can adapt across devices. Export any run as GPX, and delete the account from settings without emailing support.

Does it work around injury?

You can pause the plan and mark a session as pain, not a missed run. Tempo will not prescribe a comeback plan in the current build; it holds the week until you unpause.
