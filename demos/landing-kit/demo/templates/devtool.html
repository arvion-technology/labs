The 2am loop

The payload is the part you cannot reproduce.

Provider logs show the status code. Your app logs show the stack trace. Neither shows what was actually sent, and the delivery is already gone from the retry window by the time you look.

A tunnel that is not running

The local forward died with the laptop lid. The retry does not care, and the event is lost to the test environment.

Three dashboards, one request

The provider, the tunnel and your local server each keep a different amount of the transaction, and the timestamps disagree.

Reconstructed payloads

Hand-rolled fixtures pass against your guess and fail against the real shape, usually after the release.

Features

Three verbs, no dashboard tour.

POST /inspect

Inspect

Every delivery keeps its method, path, query, headers and raw body. Search by event id or by a field inside the JSON.

POST /replay

Replay

Send the original request to any URL, with the original headers restored. Sequential replays keep the timing between requests.

POST /diff

Diff

Compare two deliveries field by field. The common case is “works in test, fails in production”, and the diff is usually one header.

What do I install?

One static binary for the CLI, or a container image for the endpoint. The free tier uses Relay's hosted receiver; the self-hosted plan runs the same receiver in your VPC.

Do you keep the signing secret?

No. Relay stores delivery headers including the signature, but the secret used to verify it stays in your service. Redaction rules can strip any header before storage.

What happens after the retention window?

The delivery is deleted, including the body and replay fixture. Export anything you need before retention expires; the UI shows the remaining window on every delivery.
