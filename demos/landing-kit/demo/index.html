The framework

Eight sections in a fixed order. A template may omit one, but it has to say why — see the omitted column.

#SectionWhat it has to doCommon mistakeUsed by

Proof is mechanics, not logos

None of the six templates invent a customer count or a testimonial. The proof section shows something the reader can verify in a trial: a citation, an escrow flow, an engagement model.

Pricing stays visible

Five of six templates show a price. The agency template deliberately omits pricing because a fixed-scope studio sells the engagement, not a plan.

Objections before the final CTA

The last question is always the reason to leave. It is answered immediately before the repeating action, not buried under the pricing table.

Rebrand it in twelve values

Each template reads eight semantic colour tokens. Change the palette block in demo/landing.css; nothing else in the file knows what the brand is.

Palettes and measured contrast

Minimum of the four promised pairs, computed at load time in both modes.

One block per template
