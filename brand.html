Brand

The mark: an A with a fold.

Arvion needed a mark that could stand on its own at 16 pixels and still feel considered at two metres. Prism A is one letter cut into two folded planes: the left plane takes the light, the right plane falls away, and a single notch keeps the letter readable.

The seam is the ownable part. It reads as a built object rather than a drawn symbol, it survives flat printing, and it carries the idea we sell: two disciplines, design and engineering, folded into one product.

Clear space

Keep free space equal to the width of the A's left leg (about 18% of the mark height) on every side. Nothing enters that square: no text, no border, no other logo.

Minimum size

24 px / 8 mm for the monogram, 96 px / 28 mm for the horizontal lockup. Below that, use the app icon instead of the wordmark.

Do not

Do not outline it, add a gradient, rotate it, place it on a busy photograph, or rebuild the wordmark in another typeface. The mark is gold, white or black; that is the whole palette.

History

Five directions we considered

Prism A

Two folded planes and a notch. Chosen: depth, a distinctive seam, survives 16 px.

Peak A

Mountain counter with a sun. Distinct, but flatter and more literal.

Arrow A

A as an arrowhead. Energetic, but arrow marks are everywhere in software.

Orbit A

A inside a ring with a node. Elegant, but circular letter marks are crowded.

Signature A

The right leg sweeps into a signature line. Memorable, hard at small sizes.
