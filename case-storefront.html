The problem

Commerce breaks at the edges.

A cart is easy. The bugs live at the edges: a discount that must be allocated across lines without losing a paisa, a variant that sells out while the cart is open, an order that cannot move backwards without breaking the ledger.

Bazaar Store is a small commerce system that takes those edges seriously and tests them.

What we built

Money, stock and state.

  • Integer-minor-unit pricing with line discounts, promo validation and a shipping threshold.
  • Stock that reserves on cart, releases on abandonment and commits on payment.
  • An order state machine with legal transitions for pack, ship, deliver, cancel and refund.
  • Back office with revenue, low-stock alerts, inventory adjustment and an order desk.

Security and money

  • Discounts are allocated with largest-remainder so line totals sum exactly to the discounted total.
  • Stock reservation is explicit (reserve/release/commit) rather than a single decrement.
  • Payment adapters share one interface; the mock rail records a verifiable transaction id.
  • Illegal order transitions are rejected by the state machine, not by button visibility.
