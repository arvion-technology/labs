Process

How an engagement runs

01

Discovery

Two weeks, fixed price. We map the business flow, the data, the integrations and the risk before writing production code.

02

Architecture

A written architecture note you keep: system diagram, data model, failure modes, deployment and the decisions we expect to revisit.

03

Build in slices

Working software every week, behind a demo link. You can use it, and change your mind, before the scope hardens.

04

Harden

Tests, CI, security review, load checks, error handling, backups and the runbook. This is the part clients underestimate.

05

Launch and operate

Store release or deployment, monitoring, a handover session and a maintenance window if you want us to stay on.

Questions clients ask

FAQ

How long does a project take?

A focused product is typically 6–12 weeks of build after a two-week discovery. We will not promise a date before discovery, and we would rather tell you that than miss one quietly.

How do you price work?

Discovery is fixed price. The build is quoted from the architecture note — either fixed scope or a monthly team allocation. Every line item is visible; there are no surprise change orders for things we should have seen in discovery.

Who owns the code and the IP?

You do, on final payment, including the repositories and the deployment accounts. We keep the right to reuse generic patterns and libraries, and to describe the project only with your permission.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. Team extension is a normal model for us: we take ownership of a surface (web, mobile, backend, payments) and work inside your sprint process with a weekly demo.

What about payments and regulatory requirements in Nepal?

We integrate eSewa, Khalti, Stripe and Razorpay with server-to-server verification, and we have built OTP and KYC flows. Merchant accounts, licences and compliance decisions stay with you; we make the code support them.

How do you hand over?

Repository access, an architecture note, a runbook, the infrastructure as code, and a recorded walkthrough. If you want, we also stay on a maintenance window with agreed response times.

What do you not do?

We do not resell licences, build gambling or adult products, or claim AI capabilities we cannot evaluate. If a project is a bad fit we will say so in the first call.

How do we start?

Email hello@arviontech.net with what you are building and any deadline. We will propose a 30-minute call, then a written discovery proposal with scope, price and timeline.
